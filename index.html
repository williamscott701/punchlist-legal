PunchList

The defect list for a site walk: numbered tags, marked-up photos and one handover report.

Log a defect on site, photograph it, mark up exactly what is wrong with arrows and numbered pins, and hand the contractor a PDF they will accept. Everything stays on your device — there is no account and no server.
