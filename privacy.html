Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

PunchList is a defect-tracking app for construction sites, made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information. The short version: it stays on your device.

What PunchList collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-up and no server. The developer cannot see your projects, your photographs, your clients or how you use the app.

Everything you enter — projects, locations, punch items, photographs, markup, assignee names and notes — is written to storage on your own device and stays there. It is included in your device backups (iCloud or encrypted local backup) if you have those switched on, under your own Apple Account, and the developer has no access to those either.

Photographs and the camera

PunchList asks for camera access so you can photograph a defect, and for photo library access if you would rather attach a picture you already have. Images are saved into the app's own private storage on the device.

Photographs are never uploaded. They leave your device only when you deliberately export them — by generating a PDF report, exporting a CSV, or sharing a marked-up photo — and then only to wherever you send them, through the standard iOS share sheet. What happens after that is governed by whichever app or service you chose.

Location is not read from photographs, and PunchList does not request access to your location, contacts, calendar, microphone or health data.

Analytics and tracking

There are none. PunchList contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter and no third-party code that reports on you. Nothing you do in the app is measured or transmitted. There is no tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, so you will never see that prompt.

Subscriptions

The Handover Pack is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple. Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store — the app asks Apple whether a valid subscription exists and receives a yes or no. Your payment details are never seen by, or available to, the app or the developer.

Apple's own handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Network activity

The only network connections PunchList makes are to Apple's App Store, to fetch subscription prices and verify entitlements. The app is otherwise fully functional with no connection at all, which is deliberate: building sites rarely have usable signal.

Children

PunchList is a professional tool and is not directed at children. No information is knowingly collected from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or held by the developer, there is nothing to request, correct, export or erase from any server — there is no server. Under the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA and comparable laws, the data controller for the information in the app is you.

You remain in full control of the data on your device:

  • Export it with the PDF report, the CSV export, or the share sheet.
  • Delete part of it by deleting a photo, an item or a project in the app.
  • Delete all of it by deleting the app, which removes its entire container.

Deleting the app is permanent and cannot be undone by the developer. Export anything you want to keep first.
